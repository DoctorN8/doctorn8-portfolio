Rubric-based prototype
Why this feedback?

The learning coach uses the fictional Northstar Enterprise AI Use Standard. It checks for five policy-aligned considerations:

  • Data Classification and Approved Tools: Do not place raw customer or sensitive information in public tools; use only minimum necessary, approved inputs.
  • Human Review and Accountability: A designated human reviewer remains accountable before executive-facing output is shared.
  • Reliability and Source Verification: Check AI-assisted themes and summaries against available source information before relying on them.
  • Fairness, Bias, and Escalation: Check for incomplete representation, overlooked groups, or uncertainty that requires escalation.
  • Accessibility and Inclusive Communication: Present findings in clear, accessible language and avoid unsupported generalizations.
Coach boundary: This learning coach provides guidance based on a fictional policy set and a transparent learning rubric. It does not make operational, legal, security, privacy, HR, or compliance decisions.
